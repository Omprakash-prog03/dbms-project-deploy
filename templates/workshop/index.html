Why indexes matter here

Every foreign key in this schema has an index behind it, so the joins on the previous tabs do not have to scan. The two registers — sign-in history and change log — are indexed on the columns the dashboard filters by, which is why those sections stay fast as they grow.

Rule of thumb

Index the columns you filter and join on, not the ones you merely display. Every index makes a read faster and a write slightly slower, because it has to be maintained too.
